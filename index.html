[ 03 / THE STORY BEHIND THE BRAND ]
“We treat every bake as a love letter penned in Damask rosewater, Iranian saffron, and pure New Zealand pasture-fed butter.”

The Gulabi Baker was born from an obsession in Auckland: uniting the precision of luxury artisanal bakehouse craft with the royal botanical heritage of the East.

Rooted in our Glen Eden bakehouse in West Auckland, we source organic Damask roses harvested at first dawn in Kannauj, single-harvest saffron threads from Khorasan, and stone-ground emerald pistachios, folded into rich New Zealand grass-fed butter. Every sponge is rested for 24 hours to achieve sublime tenderness, and every cream is cold-steeped to preserve its delicate floral aromatic oils.
